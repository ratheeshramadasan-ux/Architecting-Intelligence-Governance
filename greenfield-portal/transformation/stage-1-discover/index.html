Stage overview

Understand the enterprise before judging its readiness.

Discovery creates one evidence-based current-state baseline with named owners, sources, validation methods, confidence and known gaps.

Stage outcomes

  • Twenty domains assessed through documented evidence.
  • Ownership, risks, constraints and missing information visible.
  • Applications, data, technology and processes traceable to business capabilities.
  • A validated baseline approved for Assess Readiness.

Evidence rules

  • No collection without a documented purpose.
  • Every record names its source and system of record.
  • Claims are confidence-rated and owner-validated.
  • Conflicts and missing information remain visible.
