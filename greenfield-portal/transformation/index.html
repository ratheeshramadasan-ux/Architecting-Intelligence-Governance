Where Should We Begin?

Transformation Entry Point & Mandate

Enterprise AI Transformation begins with executive sponsorship and clear boundary setting. Before purchasing tools or selecting models, organisations establish accountable ownership and risk appetite.

1

Mobilise Sponsorship

Establish executive sponsorship, decision rights, and funding authority.

2

Discover Baseline

Inventory current-state capabilities, systems, data, and active AI experiments.

3

Assess Readiness

Evaluate evidence gaps, capability maturity, and gap remediation priorities.

4

Define Strategy

Agree enterprise ambition, target operating model, AI principles, and roadmap.

Progression Framework

Four Phases of Enterprise AI Transformation

The fourteen stages move systematically across four governance and delivery phases.

Phase 1

Foundation & Baseline

Stages 1–3: Mobilise sponsorship, discover current state, and assess organizational readiness.

Phase 2

Strategy & Governance

Stages 4–6: Define strategy, establish governance controls, and prioritize opportunity portfolio.

Phase 3

Architecture & Delivery

Stages 7–9: Build business case, design target architecture, and plan integrated execution.

Phase 4

Execution & Scaling

Stages 10–14: Build, test, deploy, operate production, and continuously scale value.
