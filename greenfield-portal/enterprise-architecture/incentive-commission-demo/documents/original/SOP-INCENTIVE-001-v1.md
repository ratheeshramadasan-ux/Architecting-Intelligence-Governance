## 4.2 Base commission rates
Mortgage transactions earn 0.38%, personal lending 0.25%, and investments 0.18% of eligible principal. A $25 floor and $25,000 cap apply after all multipliers.

## 5.4 Customer segment factors
Premium customers receive a 1.04 multiplicative factor. New-to-bank transactions add 0.05 to the running multiplier.

## 7.1 Volume accelerators
When the dealer's eligible year-to-date volume, including the current transaction, reaches $1,000,000, add 0.08. The current transaction receives the accelerator in full.

## 8.3 Campaigns and stacking
The Spring 2026 campaign multiplies the post-segment amount by 1.08. Campaign factors apply after additive bonuses. No more than one campaign may apply.

## 9.2 Joint allocation
Divide final commission equally between eligible participants unless an approved assignment specifies percentages. Allocate rounding residue to the final listed dealer.

## 10.1 Compliance and cancellations
An ineligible dealer is placed on compliance hold. Cancellation inside 90 days creates a 100% clawback; days 91–180 create a 50% clawback.

## 11.3 Precedence and effective dates
Use transaction effective date, not processing date. Corrections retain the original transaction SOP unless an approved override states otherwise. Compliance rules override campaign, segment, and volume rules.

## 12 Worked example
A $500,000 mortgage for a premium, new-to-bank customer crossing the threshold uses 0.38%, then the premium factor, then bonuses and any eligible campaign, before caps and allocations.
