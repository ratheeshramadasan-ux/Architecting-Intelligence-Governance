AI & RPA Opportunity Prioritization

Select the right projects, expose risk and dependency constraints early, and choose technology that fits the client’s enterprise platform without turning ecosystem preference into vendor lock-in.

Decision sequence

From idea intake to an evidence-backed platform decision

The model deliberately separates opportunity value, delivery feasibility, risk exposure, and technology dependency. A high-value idea can therefore be prioritized for a controlled pilot while still being prevented from entering production until its risk and architecture conditions are satisfied.

  1. 01Discover

    Capture process volume, effort, errors, outcomes and ownership.

  2. 02Qualify

    Decide whether rules, workflow, RPA, AI assistance, decision AI or a hybrid is appropriate.

  3. 03Gate

    Assess data, security, model, resilience, architecture and change risks.

  4. 04Prioritize

    Compare the portfolio using transparent weights and risk-adjusted scores.

  5. 05Select

    Shortlist platforms using capabilities, ecosystem fit, cost, controls and portability.

  6. 06Validate

    Run proof-of-capability tests and record conditions before approval.

How the recommendation works

Do Now
Strong value and readiness with acceptable risk and dependencies.
Pilot with Controls
Promising case, but uncertainty or high-risk conditions require a time-boxed pilot and enhanced controls.
Prepare / Remediate
Value exists, but data, process, architecture or operating-model blockers must be resolved.
Defer
Weak case or disproportionate complexity; retain the rationale and reassess after material change.

Platform alignment principle

A Microsoft-heavy client should normally give Microsoft Power Platform and Copilot Studio a higher alignment score because identity, connectors, skills, operations and commercial agreements may already exist. It is not an automatic selection: mandatory capability, governance, resilience, cost and exit requirements still have to pass.

The same logic applies to AWS, Google Cloud, ServiceNow, Salesforce, SAP, IBM/Red Hat and Oracle-centred estates.
