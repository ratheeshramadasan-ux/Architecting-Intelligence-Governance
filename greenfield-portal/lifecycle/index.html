Use Case & Product Lifecycle · 14 Solution Steps

AI Solution Lifecycle

Explains how an individual AI use case, model, agent, application, or product moves from initial intake and risk tiering through engineering, evaluation, deployment, production monitoring, and eventual retirement.

Conceptual Distinction

Solution Lifecycle vs. Enterprise Transformation

While Enterprise AI Transformation establishes organizational capability, governance, and infrastructure, the AI Solution Lifecycle governs individual use cases and products through their specific engineering and operational lifecycle.

Solution Control

14 Governed Steps

Every AI use case must pass through risk classification, architecture approval, safety evaluation, and runtime monitoring before release.
