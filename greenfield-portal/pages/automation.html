Automation Framework

Enterprise governance, operating-model, and implementation guidance for intelligent automation, Robotic Process Automation (RPA), and Automation Centres of Excellence.

Executive decision summary

Automation succeeds when process ownership, exception handling, controls, support, and benefit measurement are designed with the technology.

Business outcome

Reduce avoidable manual work, improve consistency, and give employees more time for judgement and customer service.

How it works

Standardise the process, select the lowest-complexity suitable technology, design exceptions, and operate a reusable delivery model.

Decision rule

Do not automate a process that lacks a clear owner, stable inputs, measurable outcomes, or a safe exception path.

Related terms: Robotic Process Automation (RPA), RACI, and production readiness.
