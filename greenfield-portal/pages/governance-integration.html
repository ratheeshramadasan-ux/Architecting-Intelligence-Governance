AI Governance · Integrated Control System

Governance Integration Model

How leading AI, risk, regulatory, security, data, architecture, service-management, and assurance frameworks become one implementable enterprise governance system.

Executive decision summary

Use each framework for the problem it was designed to solve, then translate overlapping requirements into one control, evidence, ownership, and assurance system.

Business outcome

Reduce duplicate reviews and conflicting obligations while improving traceability for executives, regulators, and auditors.

How it works

Map authoritative obligations to shared risks, controls, lifecycle gates, owners, evidence, findings, and metrics.

Decision rule

Confirm jurisdiction and applicability with qualified legal, risk, and compliance owners; never treat a framework name as proof of compliance.

Related terms: RACI, evaluation, and control plane.

The integration problem

Use every framework for its intended target—not as another checklist

Organizations rarely lack governance guidance. They struggle because different teams interpret overlapping frameworks independently, producing duplicate controls, conflicting language, repeated evidence requests, and unclear ownership. The Ratheesh Technology model extracts the practical governance intent from each source and implements it once through a common operating model, lifecycle, control catalogue, evidence system, assurance model, and performance dashboard.

One obligation library authoritative requirements and applicabilityOne control spine reusable enterprise control objectivesOne evidence model evidence reused across obligationsOne assurance view findings, metrics, and residual risk

Official source status checked 19 July 2026. Regulatory applicability and transition dates must be confirmed for each organization, jurisdiction, role, product, and use case.

Our integration method

From external requirement to operational proof

  1. 01Interpret

    Identify the source, version, authority, scope, target outcome, and applicability trigger.

  2. 02Normalize

    Translate different terminology into a common enterprise obligation and risk language.

  3. 03Map

    Connect each obligation to business capabilities, risks, lifecycle gates, owners, and systems.

  4. 04Control

    Implement the requirement once through policy, process, architecture, technical, and human controls.

  5. 05Evidence

    Define evidence, frequency, retention, decision records, exceptions, and traceability.

  6. 06Assure

    Test design and operation through management review, second-line challenge, and independent assurance.

  7. 07Improve

    Use metrics, incidents, regulatory change, audit findings, and portfolio insight to update the system.

Public-sector governance

United States and Canadian federal-government AI governance

Government AI requirements are role- and jurisdiction-specific. The controls below apply to the identified federal institutions and activities; they should not be represented as a general law governing every private organization. Ratheesh Technology maps each requirement into the same inventory, lifecycle, control, evidence, procurement, and assurance model used across the platform.
