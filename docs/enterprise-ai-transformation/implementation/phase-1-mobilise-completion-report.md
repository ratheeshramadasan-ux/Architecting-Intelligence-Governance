# Phase 1 completion report — Mobilise

## Outcome

The approved Stage 0 — Mobilise methodology is implemented as a complete local experience suitable for a Programme Manager beginning an enterprise AI transformation. The approved lifecycle, seven-item navigation and blueprint remain unchanged.

## Pages created

- 8 canonical workstream workspaces.
- 20 canonical deliverable guidance pages.
- 6 complete workshop guides.
- 1 executive dashboard.
- 1 Programme Manager workspace.
- 1 Mobilise search page.
- 37 new canonical pages in this increment.
- 67 total validated portal pages.

## Workstreams completed

1. Executive Sponsorship
2. Business Drivers
3. Programme Governance
4. Funding & Business Ownership
5. Stakeholder Engagement
6. Operating Model
7. Risk & Compliance
8. Mobilisation Planning

Every workstream includes purpose, activities, inputs, outputs, deliverable relationships, workstream RACI, guided assistant design, references, sector considerations, pitfalls, success criteria and related stages.

## Deliverables completed

Twenty deliverables are defined and fully guided: Executive Mandate, Business Drivers, Programme Charter, Scope Definition, Strategic Objectives, Stakeholder Register, Governance Charter, Governance Forums, Decision Rights, Enterprise RACI, Delivery Model, Operating Model, Funding Model, Success Measures, AI Principles, Risk Appetite Statement, RAID Register, Mobilisation Plan, Communication Plan and Executive Briefing Pack.

Each includes business purpose, rationale, timing, owner, approver, inputs, example structure, common mistakes, guided AI-generation prompt, related deliverables, approval requirements, public/private-sector considerations and success criteria.

## Templates

Twenty downloadable Markdown template starters were generated under `/downloads/mobilise/`. Each is explicitly a blank starter, not approved evidence, and contains ownership, evidence, review, decision and approval fields.

## Workshops

Executive Vision, Business Drivers, Governance, Stakeholder, Programme Kick-off and Mobilisation Review workshops include objective, duration, participants, facilitator, preparation, agenda, discussion questions, outputs, decisions and an AI-generated-agenda prompt design.

## Guided AI assistance

Eight named assistant actions are indexed: Programme Charter, Stakeholder Register, Executive Summary, Governance Charter, Business Drivers, Success Measures, Risk Appetite and Communication Plan.

All workstream, deliverable and workshop pages expose a prompt builder. It requires enterprise context, labels missing evidence and assumptions, prevents approval inference and marks output as a human-review draft. No AI backend is connected.

## Role views

- Executive dashboard: progress, outstanding evidence, pending gate, readiness percentage, key gaps, open decisions and next milestone.
- Programme Manager workspace: activity, deliverable, RACI, workshop, decision, RAID and approval views.

Local checklist state drives planning indicators. The interface states that this is not an enterprise system of record and cannot make a gate decision.

## Traceability and validation

- 8 workstreams map to canonical deliverables.
- 20 deliverables map to owners, approvers, related deliverables and template starters.
- 6 workshops map to outputs and decisions.
- 36 Mobilise records are indexed for local search.
- Stage RACI retains exactly one accountable role and at least one responsible role per activity.
- Every canonical route resolves.
- All template routes resolve.
- The approved 14-stage lifecycle and approved navigation are automatically protected by validation.
- All 67 HTML pages pass route, heading, duplicate-ID and image-alt validation.

## Outstanding gaps

- Enterprise persistence is not connected; dashboard/checklist progress is device-local.
- The AI backend is intentionally not implemented.
- Template starters are Markdown; formatted office-document editions can be produced after content-owner review.
- Workshop scheduling, decision-log editing and RAID editing are guidance views rather than multi-user workflow.
- Formal gate approval requires authenticated records, immutable evidence and signatory capture in a later increment.
- Future animated walkthrough areas are reserved but not implemented.

## Recommendations before Phase 2 — Discover

1. Obtain content-owner review of all twenty deliverable structures and six workshops.
2. Decide which records require D1 persistence and which attachments require R2 storage.
3. Define authenticated programme instances, organisation boundaries and role-based permissions.
4. Implement immutable gate-decision and evidence-version records before using the portal as a formal system of record.
5. Convert the highest-use template starters—Programme Charter, Stakeholder Register, Enterprise RACI, RAID Register and Executive Briefing Pack—into governed office-document templates.
6. Reuse the same workstream, deliverable, workshop, dashboard, assistant and validation framework for Discover without changing the approved lifecycle.
