# Outstanding Recommendations Before Phase 4

## Enterprise decisions required

- Confirm readiness evidence thresholds and executive quorum.
- Name accountable owners for every critical gap and condition.
- Validate effort, cost and timing ranges through delivery and finance planning.
- Approve the readiness disposition and any conditions.
- Record accepted risks and escalation triggers.

## Phase 4 handoff

- Carry the Executive Readiness Decision into strategy objectives and guardrails.
- Convert approved recommendations into strategic initiatives.
- Preserve gap and evidence identifiers in roadmap and investment decisions.
- Define target maturity by capability without treating Level 5 as universally necessary.
- Keep delayed items visible in the strategic dependency register.

## Deferred by design

- Live AI execution.
- Invented enterprise scores, findings, costs, timing or approvals.
- Production deployment.
