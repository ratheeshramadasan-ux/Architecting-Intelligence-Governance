# Phase 2 — Outstanding Gaps

## Implementation gaps

No known scope gaps remain in the Phase 2 assignment. Automated portal and methodology validation and the Cloudflare deployment dry-run pass.

## Enterprise execution gaps

These are intentionally not fabricated by the platform implementation:

- named enterprise evidence owners and contributors;
- collected source evidence and extraction dates;
- evidence validation results and conflicts;
- measured confidence, freshness and completeness;
- populated inventories, assessments and findings;
- approvals for the 22 mandatory deliverables;
- the final Discovery Approval gate decision.

## Deferred by design

- Production deployment, pending explicit release authorization.
- Live AI generation, pending an approved model, security, privacy and governance integration.
- Assess Readiness implementation, which is the next approved phase and remains planned.
