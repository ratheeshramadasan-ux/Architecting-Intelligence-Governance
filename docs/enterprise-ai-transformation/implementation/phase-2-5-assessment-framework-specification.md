# Enterprise Assessment Framework Specification

## Purpose

Provide one reusable assessment structure for discovery, readiness analysis, strategy, governance, architecture, business-case decisions, implementation assurance, operations and continuous improvement.

## Canonical entities

Assessment definitions, categories, questions, scoring models, evidence requirements, confidence ratings, maturity levels, risk ratings, recommendations, improvement actions, executive summaries and AI guidance.

## Shared maturity model

1. Initial
2. Developing
3. Managed
4. Optimised
5. Enterprise Leading

## Scoring and confidence

Questions use a 0–4 evidence scale. Category averages may roll up only after minimum evidence requirements are met. Maturity and confidence are always reported separately. Confidence values are Unverified, Low, Medium and High.

## Governance rules

- Evidence precedes scoring.
- Each conclusion has one accountable owner.
- Conflicts and missing evidence remain visible.
- Recommendations trace to evidence, risks and decisions.
- AI-assisted outputs remain drafts until human validation.
- Assessments may be reused across lifecycle stages without redefining their common model.
