# Phase 3 — Roadmap Framework

## Horizons

- Immediate containment: control critical exposure before progression.
- 90 day: complete enabling actions and high-confidence quick wins.
- 6 month: deliver priority capability and control improvements.
- 12 month: complete structural investments and operating-model changes.
- 24 month: optimise and scale enterprise capability.

## Roadmap record

Each action includes its originating gap and recommendation, intended outcome, owner, dependencies, investment range, delivery confidence, target horizon, evidence of completion and approval authority.

Durations are planning horizons, not invented commitments. Delivery planning must validate dates, cost and capacity.
