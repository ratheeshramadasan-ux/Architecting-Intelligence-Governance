# Phase 3 — Executive Decision Framework

## Proceed

Evidence supports progression without material readiness conditions. Next stage: Define Strategy.

## Proceed with Conditions

Progression is justified if named conditions, owners and deadlines remain under executive oversight. Next stage: Define Strategy with controlled conditions.

## Delay

Critical gaps must be resolved before strategy work can proceed reliably. The programme remains in Assess Readiness.

## Do Not Proceed

Risk, value, feasibility or strategic alignment does not justify continued transformation. The programme is closed or reframed.

Every outcome must record business justification, risk, required actions, executive recommendation and next stage. The accountable executive forum approves the disposition; an aggregate score cannot make the decision automatically.
