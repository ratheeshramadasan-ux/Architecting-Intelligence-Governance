# 0.2 Proposed information architecture

## Five-layer model

1. Enterprise AI Transformation Lifecycle
2. Transformation Stage
3. Workstream or capability
4. Guidance or canonical knowledge page
5. Deliverable, template, checklist, tool or download

Each object has one canonical identity. Lifecycle pages explain when and why work occurs; Knowledge Centre pages explain reusable concepts; deliverables capture evidence; tools help create or assess deliverables.

## Lifecycle

| Stage | Purpose | Required exit decision |
|---|---|---|
| 0 — Mobilise | Establish mandate, scope, sponsorship, governance and investment approach. | Programme mobilisation approved |
| 1 — Discover | Establish an evidence-based current-state baseline. | Discovery baseline accepted |
| 2 — Assess Readiness | Score readiness, gaps and remediation priorities. | Readiness disposition approved |
| 3 — Define Strategy | Agree vision, ambition, outcomes, operating model and roadmap. | Enterprise AI strategy approved |
| 4 — Establish Governance | Establish policies, decision rights, controls and assurance. | Governance model approved |
| 5 — Identify and Prioritise Opportunities | Build and balance the opportunity portfolio. | Pilot and portfolio priorities approved |
| 6 — Build the Business Case | Establish benefits, costs, risk and funding. | Investment approved |
| 7 — Design the Target Architecture | Define target-state architecture and decisions. | Architecture approved |
| 8 — Plan Implementation | Create an executable, controlled delivery plan. | Implementation plan approved |
| 9 — Build and Integrate | Configure, integrate and document the solution. | Build completion approved |
| 10 — Test and Validate | Prove functional, security, AI, control and operational quality. | Release candidate accepted |
| 11 — Deploy and Transition | Cut over safely and transfer accountable ownership. | Production release approved |
| 12 — Operate and Govern Production | Operate, monitor, assure and respond to incidents. | Continued operation approved |
| 13 — Improve and Scale | Improve performance and scale reusable capability. | Scale or optimisation investment approved |

## Cross-cutting workstreams

Strategy and value; business and process; data; technology and architecture; security and identity; privacy and legal; responsible AI and governance; delivery and quality; people and change; operations and reliability; finance and procurement; vendor and third-party risk; evidence and assurance.

## Supporting platform views

- **Knowledge Centre:** glossary, canonical concepts, patterns, FAQs, standards and evidence.
- **Deliverables:** browsable by stage, workstream, role, status and approval gate.
- **Tools:** calculators, assessments, checklists and downloadable templates linked to their deliverables.
- **About:** methodology ownership, editorial standard, evidence policy and author information.
