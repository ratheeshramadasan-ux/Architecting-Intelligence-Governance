# 0.9 Standard stage-page specification

## Required structure

1. Breadcrumb and persistent lifecycle position
2. Stage number, name, purpose and plain-language outcome
3. Why the stage matters
4. Entry criteria and prerequisites
5. Questions the organisation must answer
6. Information to collect, including source and accountable validator
7. Workstreams and activities
8. Roles and decision rights
9. Deliverables with status and evidence
10. Risks, controls and dependencies
11. Stage RACI
12. Approval gate
13. Exit checklist
14. Previous and next stage

## Completion rule

A stage is not complete because its pages were read. It is complete only when mandatory deliverables exist, validation evidence is attached, checklist exceptions are recorded and the authorised approver records a gate decision.

## Accessibility and presentation

Use one `h1`, logical headings, text equivalents for visuals, keyboard-operable disclosures, labelled tables, visible focus, mobile reflow, status text that does not rely on colour, and print-friendly evidence summaries.
