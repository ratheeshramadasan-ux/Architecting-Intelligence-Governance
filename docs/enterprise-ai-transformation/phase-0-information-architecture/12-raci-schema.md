# 0.12 Standard RACI schema

## Roles

Executive Sponsor; Platform Owner; Enterprise AI Methodology Lead; Programme Director; Business Owner; Process Owner; Enterprise Architect; Solution Architect; Data Owner; Data Steward; AI/ML Lead; Engineering Lead; Security Lead; Identity Lead; Privacy Lead; Legal Lead; Risk and Compliance Lead; Procurement Lead; Finance Lead; Change Lead; Operations Lead; Service Owner; Audit and Assurance Lead.

## Assignment rules

- Exactly one Accountable role per activity or decision.
- At least one Responsible role.
- Support is distinct from Responsible.
- Consulted roles contribute before completion; Informed roles receive the decision.
- An approver cannot be inferred from an author.
- Segregation of duties applies to high-risk control validation and production release.

## Three views

- **Executive RACI:** mandates, investment, risk acceptance and gate decisions.
- **Delivery RACI:** discovery, design, build, test and transition activities.
- **Operational RACI:** service ownership, monitoring, incidents, change, assurance and continuity.
