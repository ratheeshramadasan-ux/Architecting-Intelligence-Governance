# Phase 0 executive summary

## Recommendation

Reposition Architecting AI as an **Enterprise AI Transformation Methodology**: a governed path from ambition to mobilisation, discovery, readiness, strategy, governance, opportunity selection, business case, target architecture, implementation planning, delivery, validation, production operation and continuous improvement.

The current portal contains valuable governance, architecture, security, automation and implementation material, but its navigation presents these as parallel subjects. The future model makes the lifecycle primary and retains the Knowledge Centre, Deliverables and Tools as supporting views.

## Evidence

- 28 current routes, including 24 guidance pages and four application/entry pages.
- 8 downloadable artifacts and 8 visual assets.
- No current page is proposed for deletion.
- 7 pages are retained, 8 moved, 5 merged, 7 split and 1 archived from normal navigation.
- 14 lifecycle stages are proposed, Stage 0 through Stage 13.
- 12 critical and 12 high-priority content gaps are recorded.

## Decisions requested

1. Approve the 14-stage lifecycle and the lifecycle-first navigation principle.

4. Confirm Canadian/British spelling as the editorial standard (`prioritise`, `programme`, `organisation`).
5. Approve Phase 1 to build a non-production navigation prototype with no redirects.
