# 0.13 Standard approval-gate schema

## Gate record

Gate ID and version; stage; decision question; decision owner; meeting/forum; mandatory and conditional deliverables; evidence links; control results; unresolved risks; exceptions; conditions; decision; rationale; signatories; decision time; expiry/review date; next action; audit record.

## Decisions

Approved to proceed; Approved with conditions; Rework required; Deferred; Rejected.

## Enforcement

Missing mandatory evidence defaults to `Rework required`. Approval conditions have an owner and due date. Risk acceptance identifies the accountable business or risk authority. Gate evidence is immutable after approval; corrections create a new version.
