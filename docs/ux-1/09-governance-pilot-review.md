# Governance Pilot Review

The governance pilot presents the required narrative:

Enterprise need → integrated governance system → decision rights → policies and controls → runtime enforcement → monitoring and audit → business confidence and value.

Implemented:

- Governance Wheel.
- Federated Governance Operating Model.
- Interactive Control-Layer Explorer.
- Risk Escalation Journey.
- RACI Swimlane with link to the approved detailed RACI.

The complete regulatory and theoretical source content remains immediately below the visual layer. No legal meaning, framework claim, or route has been altered.
