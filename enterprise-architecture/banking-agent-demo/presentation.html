10 · DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Enterprise agentic AI, not an LLM wrapper

  • MCP exposes narrow business capabilities, not generic SQL/admin tools.
  • The system of record and workflow state remain authoritative.
  • Identity, authorization, PII policy and approval are deterministic controls outside the prompt.
  • Every model/tool decision can be traced and evaluated.
  • Failure mode is fail-closed: no synthetic banking result when the live runtime is unavailable.
